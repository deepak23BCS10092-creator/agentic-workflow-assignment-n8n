# Problem Statement

## Target User

Students and knowledge workers who regularly handle emails, study materials, personal goals, and documents.

## Problem

Users receive information from multiple sources every day. Important tasks and deadlines are often buried inside emails, PDFs, notes, and other content.

As a result:

* Important work may be missed
* Priorities become unclear
* Study schedules are difficult to maintain
* Personal goals lose visibility
* Documents remain unread or unprocessed

Most existing tools require users to manually organize information and decide what action should be taken.

## Proposed Solution

An Agentic Personal Productivity Assistant that automatically:

1. Accepts different types of content through a single intake form.
2. Identifies the content type using an AI classification agent.
3. Routes the content to a specialized AI agent.
4. Generates structured outputs such as priorities, plans, goals, or action items.
5. Validates outputs through deterministic checks.
6. Escalates urgent items for approval.
7. Logs activities and sends notifications.

## Expected Output

Depending on the content type, the system produces:

* Prioritized emails
* Personalized study plans
* Goal progress recommendations
* Extracted action items from documents
* Email notifications
* Logged productivity records

The final result is a centralized productivity workflow that converts information into actionable outcomes.
